Cowork is now part of [Chat](/chat/). There is one kind of conversation: a chat answers a quick question as before, and
when a request needs files, commands or your computer it works through it as a [task](/chat/#tasks), in its own
[sandbox](/chat/#the-sandbox) or in a [folder](/chat/#working-in-a-folder) you give it with **+ › Add folder**, with the
[progress, background and output files panels](/chat/#while-it-works), [file cards and previews](/chat/#output-files-and-previews)
and a [permission chip](/chat/#permissions). Your Cowork tasks are listed with your chats and open as chats in their
folder, and links to `/work` lead to the chat home. A server older than this version keeps Cowork as it was until it is
updated.
