What joining means

  • The session runs on the host’s computer. You see the conversation and the changes as they happen, and you can write in it.
  • The host lets you in after comparing four emoji with you. You need no account.
  • Your messages answer with the host’s models, if they share them, or with your own. Anything that touches the host’s computer asks the host first.
  • Your computer and the host’s talk through an encrypted relay at coop.atelierhq.dev. It passes the traffic on and can’t read it. The part of this link after # never leaves your browser: this page sends nothing anywhere.
