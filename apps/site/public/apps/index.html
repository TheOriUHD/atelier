How they fit together

On their own

The desktop app and the terminal app run by themselves, with your own models and keys. There is nothing to host and no account to make.

With a server

Sign in to the same Atelier server from every app and browser, and your conversations, projects and Code sessions on your own computers are there on each one.

Extensions

The browser extensions talk to the desktop app on the same computer. What a page shows goes to the desktop app and from there to the model you chose, and nowhere else.
